KickSnatch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are

KickSnatch edits short highlight clips from livestreams (with the streamers' permission) and publishes them on our own accounts: YouTube @KickSnatch and TikTok @kicksnatchtv. Contact: snatchkick@gmail.com.

2. What data we use

- Account authorization: when the owner connects the KickSnatch account to TikTok or YouTube, we receive an OAuth access token and refresh token for that account only. We use them solely to upload and manage our own videos.
- Basic profile information of our own account (display name, username) to confirm that the connected account is ours.
- Published video identifiers and status, so that we do not post the same video twice.
We do not collect personal data from visitors or viewers, and we do not run an app for the public.

3. Where data is stored

Tokens and video identifiers are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account connection is active. The owner can disconnect at any time and revoke access in the TikTok or Google account settings, after which the stored tokens are deleted. To ask for deletion of any data, email snatchkick@gmail.com.

5. Children

KickSnatch is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
